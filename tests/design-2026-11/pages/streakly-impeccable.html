    Questions, answered.

    How many grace days do I get?

    Grace days are built to be generous enough for real family life. You choose how many to keep in reserve per habit, so you decide how forgiving each streak should be.

    Is it just for me, or can my partner join?

    Both. Keep private habits to yourself and share the family ones with a co-parent or caregiver. Everyone sees only what you’ve shared.

    How long does a check-in take?

    One tap from the notification or home-screen widget. If you can open a text, you can check in.
