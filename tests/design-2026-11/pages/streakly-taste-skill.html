Questions parents ask

How many habits can I track?

Three to start. You can add more later from the habits list.

What happens when I miss a day?

A grace day covers it. Without one, the streak resets, and your longest streak stays on record.

Can my partner use it too?

Yes. Share any habit and each of you checks in on your own phone.

Do the kids need an account?

No. Streakly is for the adults; children appear only as names on a shared habit.
